Hermes — Privacy Policy

Hermes is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

  • Hermes accesses the owner's own Gmail account in read-only mode, with the owner's explicit consent, to detect new messages.
  • All processing happens locally on the owner's personal computer. Email data is not stored on remote servers, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • The only data transmitted elsewhere is a notification to the owner's own Telegram account.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
